UoCode is an intelligent model service platform designed for general users, creators, office professionals, and advanced AI users. We aim to simplify the complex process of integrating AI models, enabling more people to easily access high-quality AI models such as Claude Opus, Codex GPT, and GPT-5, and truly apply AI to learning, work, creation, and daily efficiency improvement. In the past, experiencing different AI models often required registering on multiple platforms separately, understanding different usage rules, handling complex interface configurations, and switching back and forth between multiple services. For ordinary users, this is not only troublesome but also increases the barrier to entry. What UoCode wants to do is simple: bring together excellent AI models and provide a unified, clear, and user-friendly entry point. Whether you want to use AI to write articles, summarize materials, translate content, analyze documents, or assist in programming, build tools, and optimize workflows, you can more conveniently find the right model capabilities through UoCode. Why we built UoCode AI is changing the way many people work, but truly useful AI models are not always easy to obtain. Many users know that models like Claude Opus, Codex GPT, GPT-5.4, and GPT-5.5 are powerful, but do not know how to use them stably and conveniently. Some users just want to write a high-quality article but are blocked by complex model names and interface configurations; some users want to use stronger models to assist in code development but have to switch repeatedly between different platforms; and some teams hope to centrally manage AI usage but lack a simple and intuitive tool.
