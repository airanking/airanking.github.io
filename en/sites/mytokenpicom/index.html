MyTokenPi is a unified AI model aggregation and distribution gateway for individual developers, AI application teams, and enterprise users. Its core positioning is to integrate large language models from different sources and in different formats, converting them into compatible interfaces such as OpenAI, Claude, and Gemini, allowing users to manage calls to multiple AI models with one key and one interface. Key advantages: MyTokenPi's advantages are "unified integration, unified management, and compatibility with mainstream interfaces." Users do not need to integrate with multiple model platforms separately; they can centrally call multiple models through one gateway, reducing integration costs and maintenance complexity. Developers already accustomed to OpenAI API calls can also migrate to or expand into model ecosystems such as Claude and Gemini more smoothly. The site also emphasizes discounted pricing: every model has a discount of 1-9 tenths of the official price, and users are directed to /pricing for details. This indicates that the platform focuses on offering more flexible and cost-effective model calls than direct official access. Service features: MyTokenPi provides model aggregation, interface format conversion, API key acquisition, top-ups, and a model marketplace/price lookup. It is an AI API relay and distribution platform suitable for chatbots, AI tools, content generation, intelligent customer service, coding assistants, and internal enterprise AI applications. The platform supports OpenAI-compatible, Claude-compatible, and Gemini-compatible interface formats, making it developer-friendly and reducing the work required to adapt to different model SDKs and interface specifications. It has also established an in-depth research collaboration mechanism with Zhejiang University; multiple laboratories have connected to our API, and there are 500 on-campus users. Pricing information: MyTokenPi offers model price discounts marked as 1-9 tenths of official prices. Billing for specific models is subject to the site's pricing page: mytokenpi.com/pricing. It focuses on kiro (high cache), official account pools (full capability), and official api-key. The homepage allows users to contact customer service to receive free trial credit.
