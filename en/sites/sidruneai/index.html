sidrune: Current public-data score: 67.5, with 85% field coverage. Uptime 98.2%; latency 6.8 s. Sidrune AI is a multi-model AI access and management platform for enterprise users and individual developers. Through one account and a unified API, users can access currently available models such as GPT, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, GLM, and Kimi, without separately managing accounts, keys, and bills for each model. The platform provides self-operated account pools and multiple unofficial API routes. Users can choose between price-priority and stability-priority routes based on their budget and use cases. Sidrune AI is compatible with commonly used OpenAI and Anthropic interfaces, can connect to AI coding tools and clients that support custom interfaces, and can also be used through SDKs for applications, scripts, and automated workflows. Multi-route scheduling is intended to reduce the impact of fluctuations in any single route, but actual availability remains subject to the status of each route. The model marketplace publicly displays current models, routes, and prices; the workspace provides invocation usage and cost records to help users verify spending. The platform supports usage-based balances and subscription plans, and provides RMB settlement, Chinese guides, and support. Models, prices, and available routes are subject to real-time information in the official website's model marketplace.
