We aim to simplify the complex process of integrating AI models, enabling more people to easily access high-quality AI models such as Claude Opus, Codex GPT, and GPT-5, and truly apply AI to learning, work, creation, and daily efficiency improvement. In the past, experiencing different AI models often required registering on multiple platforms separately, understanding different usage rules, handling complex interface configurations, and switching back and forth between multiple services. Some users just want to write a high-quality article but are blocked by complex model names and interface configurations; some users want to use stronger models to assist in code development but have to switch repeatedly between different platforms; and some teams hope to centrally manage AI usage but lack a simple and intuitive tool.
