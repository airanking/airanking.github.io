AI Ranking

Choose evidence before an API provider

AI Ranking is a public-data index, not a laboratory benchmark. Use its listings to build a shortlist, identify missing information, and prepare your own checks. A high position does not establish model quality, security, or suitability for your workload.

Start with your actual use case

A small prototype

Prioritize a low spending limit, understandable documentation, and easy key revocation. Test one representative request before funding an account.

An interactive product

Check streaming, peak-hour errors, and time to the first useful response. A published latency figure cannot describe the whole user experience.

A production workflow

Require written data-handling terms, operational contacts, and a tested fallback. Treat purchasing, privacy, and technical acceptance as separate decisions.

Questions before you shortlist

Does AI Ranking test every provider?

No. The index organizes public records; its score is not an independent performance benchmark or a security assessment.

Does a higher score mean better answers?

No. The score combines listing signals, not task accuracy. Evaluate output quality with examples and acceptance criteria relevant to your application.

Are uptime and latency directly comparable?

Not necessarily. Records are snapshots with an unknown measurement window; regions, request types, and collection methods may differ.

Does a topic listing confirm model support?

No. Topics use keyword matches, not capability verification. Confirm exact models, versions, features, and access restrictions with the operator.

Is missing information a failed check?

No. Missing signals are omitted from the weighted mean and reduce coverage. They remain questions to investigate, not proof of failure.

Can I rely on a refund or invoice badge?

Only as a lead. Obtain the current written terms, applicable legal entity, eligibility conditions, and any deadlines before paying.
